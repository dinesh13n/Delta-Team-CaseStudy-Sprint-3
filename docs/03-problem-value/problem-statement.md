Classification: **[VF]** Verified Fact, **[INF]** Inference, **[ASM]** Assumption, **[UNK]** Unknown.

Operations staff who manage shipments, vehicles, routes, carrier bookings and delivery exceptions cannot rely on the information they are given, and the business cannot show afterwards who decided what or why.

Records can be duplicated, incomplete or confused with records of a different kind. Some records are returned when the wanted one does not exist. Access to information depends on a label the requester supplies about themselves. Decisions leave a trail that does not say who acted or which request it belonged to, and about one third of recorded business events cannot be linked to a request at all.

The result is slower exception handling, higher risk of wrong action on a shipment, and an inability to demonstrate control to auditors and customers. The problem to solve is to make operational information trustworthy, access to it proportionate to the person and purpose, and every decision traceable.
